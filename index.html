HoboLife Environmental Access Initiative

A community-driven environmental health system designed to reduce exposure risks, improve neighborhood conditions, and create low-barrier micro-work opportunities for unhoused residents across Colorado.

About the Initiative

The HoboLife Environmental Access Initiative (HEAI) addresses a critical gap: unhoused residents face the highest exposure to heat, air pollution, contaminated sites, illegal dumping, and hazardous waste — yet they remain excluded from environmental decision-making. HEAI flips this dynamic by placing community members at the center of environmental monitoring, response, and improvement.

How It Works

Hazard Reporting

Community members report environmental hazards — trash, needles, spills, heat risks, stormwater issues — through the HoboLife platform. Reports populate a live environmental map shared with partners.

Exposure Reduction

Real-time alerts for air quality, heat index, severe weather, and safe routes help reduce environmental exposure for unhoused residents.

Gig-Based Micro-Work

Cleanup tasks are posted, completed, verified, and compensated through a low-barrier micro-work system designed for accessibility and dignity.

Environmental Data Integration

HEAI integrates AQI, heat index, DI community overlays, hazard reports, and cleanup metrics to support smarter decision-making and targeted interventions.

Measurable Impact

HEAI tracks and reports environmental improvements in real time, creating a transparent, enforcement-grade dataset ideal for SEPs, grants, and municipal partnerships.
